**What's there**

*Base* — Sun, eight planets and all 42,075 NEOs positioned by solving each body's own Kepler orbit from its own epoch. Verified against reality: Earth's ecliptic longitude on 2026‑07‑24 puts the Sun at 120.7° (correct for that date), Halley's orbit comes out retrograde at 35.2 au near aphelion with perihelion Feb 1986. Time plays/pauses/reverses from minutes-per-second to years-per-second, scrubs across 1900–2199, or jumps to a date.

The 42k objects are handled by solving Kepler **in the vertex shader** — orbital elements are vertex attributes, time is one uniform, so the whole catalogue re-propagates every frame and filtering is an attribute flip. Epochs are stored as days from J2000 rather than raw JD, which matters: a JD near 2.46e6 would eat the entire float32 mantissa.

*Directions, all implemented* — search + eight filters (PHA, Sentry, named, has-approach, class chips, diameter, MOID, semi-major axis); detail panel for any object with orbit, physical data, live Sun/Earth distance and a clickable close-approach table that jumps the clock; Sentry impact-risk section (probability as 1-in-N, Palermo, Torino, impact window) plus a risk leaderboard; a live "next close approaches" list that tracks the clock; four colour encodings with legends; comets with three propagation paths (elliptic from `ma`/`n`, hyperbolic Kepler from `tp`, Barker's equation for the 1,762 parabolic ones with no `a` or `ma`); focus-and-follow; and full deep links.

**Two things worth knowing** (both in `README.md`): the dataset stores elements to ~4 significant figures, and mean motion is the sensitive one — for Apophis that alone puts the 2029 encounter about a million km off along-track, so positions are geometrically right but not ephemeris-grade. And only 1,264 of 42,075 NEOs have a measured diameter; the rest are estimated from H with an assumed 0.14 albedo, marked as estimates in the UI.

Build-time preprocessing (`scripts/prepare-data.mjs`) repacks the 27 MB of source JSON into ~7 MB of mostly-binary columnar blobs in `public/data/` → `dist/data/`; `data/` itself is never touched.
