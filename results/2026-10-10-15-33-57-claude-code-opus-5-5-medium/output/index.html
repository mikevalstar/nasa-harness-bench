    NEO Orrery

    Every point is a near-Earth object placed by solving its orbit (two-body Kepler motion from JPL orbital elements) for the date shown at the bottom. Scrub or play time to watch them move.

    • Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.
    • Click an object for details, double-click to follow it.
    • Space play/pause · [ ] speed · F follow · / search · Esc clear

    Positions far from the elements' epoch ignore planetary perturbations, so they drift from reality over decades; recorded close approaches come from JPL's full numerical integration. Planets and the Sun are drawn larger than life; zoom in to see their true size. 1 LD = Earth–Moon distance (384,400 km).
